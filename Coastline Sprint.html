Coastline Sprint

Seven laps per level, and there is no speed limit. Every level is a new location with a new road and more traffic. Stay on the tarmac and beat your best time.

Arrow keys or WASD to drive. Space or F fires a homing missile at cars ahead; you get 5, and they recharge. Rivals fire back once every 30 seconds. You have 5 hearts and lose one each time you hit a car. On touch, use the on-screen pads.
